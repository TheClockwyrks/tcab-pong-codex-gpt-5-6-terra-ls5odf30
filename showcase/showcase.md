# Carom

Carom starts with a clean serve across a midnight field: cyan on the left,
pink on the right, and a pair of gold pylons turning every rally into a puzzle.
The ball is quick, but the court is built for a well-placed bank shot.

The trick is the paddle motion. Slide into the ball and it leaves with spin,
curling its path after the hit. A defensive save can become a wicked curve that
wraps around a pylon and catches an opponent moving the wrong way.

Solo pits you against a reactive opponent; Versus makes the same keyboard a
tiny local arena. Every point resets the tension, and every match point invites
one more impossible angle.
